This Privacy Policy explains how the Mandarin Helper mobile application for Android (the “App”) handles information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer of Mandarin Helper. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and we do not operate any servers that receive your data.

  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you choose to sign in, Google stores your cloud save, achievements and leaderboard scores under its own privacy policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it does not require you to create an account with us.

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and we do not use device identifiers to track you across apps or websites.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the App work, the following information is saved in the App’s private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access it, and it is never sent to us.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services provided by Google. When you use these features, Google processes information under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases of additional HSK levels are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment information; we do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details. The App only receives confirmation of which products you own so that it can unlock them. This confirmation is checked with Google Play so that purchases can be restored on a new device.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

You may choose to sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, the App uses it to:

  • Cloud save: store a copy of your learning progress in your Google account so it can be restored on another device;
  • Achievements and leaderboards: record achievements and submit game scores, which may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings.

This information is stored by Google, not by us. Google may also process your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and avatar). If you do not sign in, none of this information is shared and the App works normally.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation is played using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The App sends only the Chinese word or sentence to be spoken. Speech is generated by your device’s speech engine, whose provider’s privacy policy applies.

Google Play Store

If you choose to rate the App, a link opens the App’s page in the Google Play Store. Any review you post is handled by Google.

4. How information is used

Information stored on your device is used only to provide the App’s features: saving your progress, scheduling reviews, showing statistics and badges, unlocking purchased content and remembering your settings. We do not use any information for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell or rent information to anyone.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Reset progress: open Settings › Progress › Reset progress to clear your learning progress, statistics, streaks and badges. Purchases are kept.
  • Remove all data: clear the App’s storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Google Play Games data: if you signed in, you can manage or delete your Play Games data, including cloud saves, in the Google Play Games settings for your Google account.

Your purchases remain linked to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling the App.

6. Data security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system. Communication with Google Play services is encrypted by Google. Because we do not collect or hold your data, there is no copy of it on our side that could be exposed. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we also recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.

7. Children’s privacy

Mandarin Helper is designed to be suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The App contains no advertising and no social features that allow users to contact each other. In-app purchases and Google Play Games sign-in are provided by Google and are subject to the parental controls and family settings available in Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, we hold no data to access, correct or delete. All App data is under your control on your device, as described in Section 5. For information held by Google, please use the tools Google provides or contact Google directly.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

9. App permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs:

  • Internet and network state: to connect to Google Play for purchases, restoring purchases and the optional Google Play Games features.
  • Vibration: for the optional vibration feedback on right and wrong answers, which can be turned off in Settings.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

10. International users

We do not transfer your information anywhere, because it stays on your device. If you use Google Play services, Google may process information in countries other than your own, as described in Google’s privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, we will let you know through the App or its Google Play listing. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
