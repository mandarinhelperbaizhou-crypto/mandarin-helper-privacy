Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mandarin Helper mobile application for Android (the “App”) handles information. Mandarin Helper is developed and published on Google Play by Eternal-T Games. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Eternal-T Games, the developer of Mandarin Helper. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and we do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • Your learning progress and settings are stored on your device. Copies are kept in your own Google account only if you use Android backup or sign in with Google Play Games, never with us.
  • The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The microphone is used only if you choose speaking practice in Flashcards. Recordings stay in your phone’s memory and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you choose to sign in, Google stores your cloud save (progress and settings), achievements and leaderboard scores under its own privacy policy.
  • To check for app updates, the App downloads a small file from GitHub Pages. Nothing about you is sent, but GitHub can see your IP address, as with any web request.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it does not require you to create an account with us. The microphone is used only for optional speaking practice, and recordings never leave your device (see Speaking practice).

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and we do not use device identifiers to track you across apps or websites.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the App work, the following information is saved in the App’s private storage on your device. Other apps cannot access it, and it is never sent to us. The App also keeps a backup copy of your previous save in the same private storage, so your progress can be recovered if the save file is ever damaged.

Speaking practice (microphone)

In Flashcards you can tap 🎤 to record yourself saying a word for 3 seconds and compare it with the App’s voice, for example to practise tones. The App asks for microphone permission the first time you do this. The recording is kept only in your phone’s memory while it plays back. It is never saved to storage or sent anywhere, and it is gone when you move to the next card or leave the screen. You can turn the permission off at any time in your device settings; the rest of the App works without it.

口语练习（麦克风）：在单词卡中，你可以点 🎤 录下自己读单词的声音（3 秒），并和应用的标准发音比较，比如用来练习声调。第一次使用时，应用会请求麦克风权限。录音只在播放时暂存在手机内存中，不会被保存或发送到任何地方，切换到下一张卡片或离开页面后就会消失。你可以随时在手机设置中关闭该权限，应用的其他功能不受影响。

Progress report

On the Stats screen you can share a short text summary of your progress (words mastered, study time, streak and HSK levels). It is created on your device and is sent only when you tap Share, and only to the app or person you choose. On devices without a share option it is copied to your clipboard instead.

学习报告：在统计页面，你可以分享一份简短的学习进度总结（已掌握的单词、学习时间、连续学习天数和 HSK 等级）。报告在你的设备上生成，只有在你点“分享”时才会发送，而且只发送给你选择的应用或联系人。在没有分享功能的设备上，报告会复制到剪贴板。

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services provided by Google, and on GitHub Pages for its update check. When you use these features, Google processes information under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy; GitHub’s privacy statement is linked below.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases of additional HSK levels are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment information; we do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details. The App only receives confirmation of which products you own so that it can unlock them. This confirmation is checked with Google Play so that purchases can be restored on a new device.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

You may choose to sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, the App uses it to:

  • Cloud save: store a copy of your learning progress and settings (such as sound, theme, language, daily goal and which tutorials you have seen) in your Google account so they can be restored after reinstalling the App or on another device;
  • Achievements and leaderboards: record achievements and submit game scores, which may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings.

This information is stored by Google, not by us. Google may also process your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and avatar). If you do not sign in, none of this information is shared and the App works normally.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your Google account on your device, Android automatically backs up the App’s data (your progress and settings) to your Google account and restores it when you reinstall the App or set up a new device. This backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off or manage it in your device’s backup settings.

Update check (GitHub Pages)

When you open the App, it downloads a small file from GitHub Pages (pyros-gm.github.io), a web hosting service run by GitHub, Inc., to find out whether a newer version of the App is available on Google Play. The request contains no information about you or your progress. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device’s IP address and basic technical details of the request, which it may log to operate and secure its service under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive this information.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation is played using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The App sends only the Chinese word or sentence to be spoken. Speech is generated by your device’s speech engine, whose provider’s privacy policy applies.

Google Play Store

If you choose to rate the App, a link opens the App’s page in the Google Play Store. Any review you post is handled by Google.

4. How information is used

Information stored on your device is used only to provide the App’s features: saving your progress, scheduling reviews, showing statistics and badges, unlocking purchased content, remembering your settings and checking whether an update is available. We do not use any information for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell or rent information to anyone.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Reset progress: open Settings › Progress › Reset progress to clear your learning progress, statistics, streaks and badges. Purchases are kept.
  • Remove all data: clear the App’s storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App. If Android backup is on, a backup copy may remain in your Google account until you delete it in your Google account’s backup settings or turn backup off.
  • Google Play Games data: if you signed in, you can manage or delete your Play Games data, including cloud saves, in the Google Play Games settings for your Google account.

Your purchases remain linked to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling the App.

6. Data security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system. Communication with Google Play services is encrypted by Google, and the update check uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Backups made by Android and Google Play Games are stored and protected by Google in your Google account. Because we do not collect or hold your data, there is no copy of it on our side that could be exposed. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we also recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.

7. Children’s privacy

Mandarin Helper is designed to be suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The App contains no advertising and no social features that allow users to contact each other. In-app purchases and Google Play Games sign-in are provided by Google and are subject to the parental controls and family settings available in Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, we hold no data to access, correct or delete. All App data is under your control on your device, as described in Section 5. For information held by Google or GitHub, please use the tools they provide or contact them directly.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

9. App permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs:

  • Internet and network state: to connect to Google Play for purchases, restoring purchases and the optional Google Play Games features, and to check GitHub Pages for app updates.
  • Vibration: for the optional vibration feedback on right and wrong answers, which can be turned off in Settings.
  • Microphone (record audio): only for the optional speaking practice in Flashcards. Android asks you the first time you tap 🎤, and you can refuse or turn it off later. Recordings never leave your device (see Speaking practice).

The App does not access your camera, location, contacts, photos or files.

10. International users

We do not receive or transfer your information ourselves. If you use Android backup or Google Play services, Google may process information in countries other than your own, as described in Google’s privacy policy. GitHub may process the technical details of the update check in the United States and other countries, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
